Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

In short: Whole Page Screenshot does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It captures a web page only when you ask it to, and the picture stays on your device.

Who we are

Whole Page Screenshot for Safari is published by Dmitry Skorinko, an individual developer, through the App Store, for Mac, iPhone and iPad.

What the extension sees

Whole Page Screenshot captures a web page only when you ask it to: by clicking its toolbar button, pressing its keyboard shortcut, or choosing it from the right-click menu. Safari then gives it access to that one page, in that one tab, until the tab moves to another page. It does not read pages in the background and has no access to your browsing history.

To capture the whole page, the extension scrolls it from top to bottom and takes a screenshot of each screen, then puts the page back where you were. It doesn’t read or keep the page’s text.

What is stored

The screenshots are kept in the extension’s own storage on your device so the editor can show them, and deleted after a day. Your options, such as the file format and resolution, are kept there too. Nothing leaves your device unless you save, share or copy a picture yourself. Removing the app removes everything it stored.

Network requests

Whole Page Screenshot makes no requests to the developer or to any third party. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code.

Purchases

Whole Page Screenshot is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and the developer does not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

Whole Page Screenshot collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
